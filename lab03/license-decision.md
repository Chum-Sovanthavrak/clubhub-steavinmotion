# License decision · ITC Club Hub
Version 1.0 · 2026-11-12 · Lead: Chum Sovanthavrak <Scrum Master>

| Criterion | MIT | Apache-2.0 | GPL-3.0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Anyone (incl. companies) may reuse | Yes | Yes | Yes (must also use GPL-3.0) |
| Improvements must be shared back | No | No | Yes (strong copyleft) |
| Explicit patent grant | No | Yes | Yes |
| Compatible with GoogleTest (BSD-3) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Simple for future student teams | Yes (very short and clear) | Moderate (longer text, NOTICE rules) | Complex (strict copyleft obligations) |
| Instructor / institute requirement | Meets open-source guidelines | Meets open-source guidelines | Meets open-source guidelines |

**Decision:** MIT License

**Rationale:** We chose the MIT License because it is brief, easy for future student teams to read and understand, and imposes minimal legal friction on anyone reusing or building upon ITC Club Hub. Unlike GPL-3.0, it does not force derivative works to adopt a specific copyleft licence, and unlike Apache-2.0, it avoids extra administrative requirements like maintaining a NOTICE file while remaining fully compatible with our BSD-3-Clause test dependency (GoogleTest).
